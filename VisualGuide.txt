# THE DEEP CURRENT
## Personal Color & Visual Identity Guide
**Version 1.0**

### 1. Core identity

The Deep Current is a visual identity inspired by oceanic depth, intellectual curiosity, modern technology, exploration and creative freedom.

It combines vibrant aquatic blues with deep navy tones, muted natural colors and restrained warm accents.

The identity should communicate:

- Intelligence without corporate coldness.
- Modernity without looking futuristic or artificial.
- Creativity without visual chaos.
- Freshness without excessive brightness.
- Sophistication without pretentiousness.
- Exploration, depth and curiosity.

**Personality keywords:** Curious, analytical, creative, adventurous, observant, modern, thoughtful, independent.

**Central motto:** There is always more beneath the surface.

### 2. Official color palette

Preserve these HEX values exactly.

| Color | HEX | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Current Blue | `#0095B6` | Signature primary color |
| Abyss | `#082B36` | Deep backgrounds and contrast |
| Ocean Navy | `#102F46` | Structure and sophistication |
| Petrol | `#145766` | Secondary aquatic color |
| Sea Glass | `#A8D0CC` | Soft accents and surfaces |
| Arctic Mist | `#DCEBE8` | Light backgrounds |
| Warm Ivory | `#F3EEE3` | Typography and warmth |
| Stone | `#C9B69B` | Natural neutral |
| Coral | `#D97757` | Warm expressive accent |
| Amber | `#C69A3A` | Selective highlights |

### 3. Color distribution

Recommended proportions:

- 35% — Abyss and Ocean Navy.
- 25% — Arctic Mist and Warm Ivory.
- 20% — Current Blue.
- 10% — Petrol and Sea Glass.
- 10% — Coral and Amber.

These proportions are guidelines, not strict requirements.

Current Blue should remain recognizable as the signature color.

### 4. Visual direction

**Preferred aesthetic:**

- Modern editorial design.
- Aquatic and oceanic visual references.
- Contemporary Japanese design.
- Minimalist architecture.
- Cinematic photography.
- Modern scientific and technological aesthetics.
- Clean geometric compositions.
- Subtle gradients inspired by water and atmospheric light.

**Typography:**

- Contemporary sans-serif fonts for primary headings.
- Elegant serif fonts for editorial contrast.
- Clean, readable typography for technical content.
- Generous spacing and intentional hierarchy.

### 5. Textures and materials

Preferred visual textures:

- Deep ocean water.
- Glass and translucent materials.
- Brushed aluminum.
- Matte surfaces.
- Natural stone.
- Soft photographic grain.
- Reflections and atmospheric lighting.
- Subtle gradients and layered surfaces.

### 6. Recurring visual references

The identity draws inspiration from:

- Ocean depths and underwater photography.
- Modern architecture.
- Science, mathematics and technology.
- Japanese graphic design.
- Contemporary editorial photography.
- Aquatic landscapes and coastal environments.
- Cinematic blue-hour photography.
- Minimalist industrial design.

These references are optional sources of inspiration, not mandatory design elements.

### 7. What to avoid

Unless explicitly requested, avoid:

- Generic corporate turquoise branding.
- Excessive neon cyan.
- Overly saturated gradients.
- Generic cryptocurrency aesthetics.
- Excessive glassmorphism.
- Cartoonish aquatic illustrations.
- Excessive tropical or beach-themed imagery.
- Unnecessary glowing effects.
- Overuse of warm accent colors.

### 8. Instructions for AI systems

When generating visual material using this identity:

1. Use `#0095B6` as the defining signature color.
2. Preserve all official HEX values.
3. Build depth using Abyss, Ocean Navy and Petrol.
4. Use Arctic Mist and Warm Ivory to introduce breathing room.
5. Use Coral and Amber sparingly.
6. Maintain excellent readability and contrast.
7. Adapt the palette to the medium and purpose.
8. Do not force every palette color into every composition.
9. Maintain consistency across related designs.
10. Prioritize atmosphere, composition and intentional color relationships.

### 9. Final creative principle

**The Deep Current is about what lies beneath the surface.**

It represents curiosity, depth, exploration and the balance between analytical thinking and creative expression.

Every design should feel contemporary, intelligent, fluid and quietly distinctive.
